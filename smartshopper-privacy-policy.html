SmartShopper Privacy Policy

Developer: June Hong
Android app: com.smartshopper777
Effective date: November 8, 2026

This policy describes how SmartShopper for Android handles shopping information, camera access and information processed by its third-party services.

Shopping lists and membership cards

SmartShopper stores shopping-list names, items, quantities, notes, categories and purchase status in a local database on your device. It also stores membership-card names, barcode values, barcode formats and notes. SmartShopper has no developer-operated account, server or cloud-sync service, and the developer does not receive your lists or cards. Third-party SDK processing and Android backup are separate from this local storage.

Camera and barcode scanning

The app requests camera permission when you choose to scan a membership-card barcode. The app processes camera frames using Google ML Kit on the device and saves the selected barcode value and format, rather than a camera photo, to the local card record. You can also enter a barcode manually without scanning. ML Kit collects diagnostic and usage information as described below.

Advertising

SmartShopper displays banner ads using Google Mobile Ads. Google's SDK disclosure lists automatic collection and sharing of IP addresses, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. IP addresses may be used to estimate general location. The specific information processed depends on SDK version, settings, available identifiers and consent choices. Limited ads do not mean that no data is processed.

See Google Mobile Ads data disclosure for the provider's description.

ML Kit diagnostics and usage information

Google ML Kit's disclosure lists device information (such as model and operating system), app package and version, per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration, feature input/output sizes, feature versions, event types and error codes for diagnostics and usage analytics. This SDK information is distinct from the barcode value stored in your membership-card record.

See ML Kit's Android data disclosure for details and any feature-specific processing.

Privacy choices and permissions

SmartShopper uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request updated consent information and display a consent form where required. Starting with version 2.0.2, the menu (⋮) on the home screen links to this policy and shows Privacy options, where you can review or change your consent choices, when Google reports that this entry point is required in your region.

You can manage camera permission and advertising-ID controls through Android settings. Available controls and names vary by Android version. Changing a permission or consent choice does not itself delete information already processed by third-party services.

Sending feedback

Starting with version 2.0.2, the home screen menu includes Send feedback. It opens your own email app with a message addressed to the developer, prefilled with the app version, device model and Android version. The app itself sends nothing. If you choose to send the email, the developer receives your email address, the message and any details you leave in it, and uses them only to respond and to improve the app. You can edit or remove the prefilled details before sending.

Storage, backup and deletion

You can delete shopping lists and membership cards in the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app data, subject to Android backup and restore behavior. The app allows Android backup with Android's default rules. Depending on your device and settings, app data may be backed up to your Google account or transferred to a new device and later restored. This policy does not promise that data never leaves your device.

Information processed by Google services follows the provider's retention and deletion practices. This policy does not promise a particular retention period or immediate deletion of third-party records.

Intended audience

SmartShopper is designed for adults managing grocery shopping. It is not designed as a child-directed app.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the app's data practices change, and the effective date above will change with it.
