Shadow Speak Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Shadow Speak ("the app") is an English shadowing speaking-practice app developed by June Hong. This policy explains what information the app handles, and what it does not.

Overview

The app works without an account and without a server of its own. You do not sign in, and the developer does not receive your practice data. Your recordings, scores and settings stay on your device, except for the single case described under "Pronunciation scoring" below — and that case is under your control.

Information we do not collect

The app does not collect, and we do not receive, any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact details
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, or files
  • Your voice recordings — except when you explicitly request a score, as described below

Because there is no account and no developer server, there is no user profile for us to store, share, or delete.

Information stored on your device only

  • Your practice settings (for example playback speed, repetitions, and whether ads are shown)
  • Your most recent practice take (recording). Only the latest take is kept; it is deleted when you record a new one or leave the Practice screen
  • Your Microsoft Azure subscription key and region, if you choose to enter them for pronunciation scoring. They are kept in your device's secure storage, are never shown in the app after saving, and are used only to send scoring requests to your own Azure account

This data is never transmitted to us. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it — uninstalling the app also removes your saved Azure key.

Microphone and recordings

The app uses the microphone only when you record a practice take. Recordings are stored temporarily on your device for playback comparison and scoring, and are handled as described above.

Pronunciation scoring (optional, your own Azure account)

Pronunciation scoring is optional. If you enter your own Microsoft Azure Speech key in Settings and tap Score my take, the app sends that take's audio and the sentence text directly from your device to Microsoft's Azure Speech Pronunciation Assessment service, using your key and your Azure account. The score is returned to the app and shown to you.

Nothing is sent anywhere when you do not tap Score my take. The developer does not receive your audio, your scores, or your Azure key, and has no access to your Azure account. Microsoft's handling of the audio you send is described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Advertising

The app can show a single banner ad at the bottom of the Library screen, provided by Google AdMob. Ads are off by default and can be turned on or off at any time in Settings (Show ads). The app requests non-personalized ads only; we do not provide Google with any personal information about you for ad personalization.

As the ad provider, Google may collect limited device information in connection with serving and measuring ads, including for fraud prevention, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and the AdMob data disclosure. We use no other advertising or analytics service; the only statistics available to us are the aggregated, anonymized app vitals provided by Google Play, which do not identify you.

Children

The app is a general-audience English practice tool. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from anyone else: there is no account, no chat, and no social feature. A parent or guardian who wishes to remove all locally stored data can do so by deleting the app from the device.

Data retention and deletion

Because app data is stored only on your device, we hold no copy of it and there is nothing for us to delete on a server. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data from the device. If you contact us about this policy, any email you send is handled only to answer your question and is not added to any marketing list.
