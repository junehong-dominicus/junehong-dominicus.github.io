Senior Fit Coach Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Senior Fit Coach ("the app") is a workout reminder and alarm app developed by June Hong. This policy explains what information the app handles, and what it does not.

Overview

The app works without an account and without a server of its own. You do not sign in to use the app, and the developer does not receive your reminders, workout history, or settings. Everything the app stores stays on your device, except for the single optional case described under "YouTube connection" below — and that connection is entirely up to you.

Information we do not collect

The app does not collect, and we do not receive, any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact details
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, or files
  • Health or fitness data from HealthKit or any other health service

Because there is no account and no developer server, there is no user profile for us to store, share, or delete. The app contains no advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Information stored on your device only

  • Your reminders — the times and repeat patterns you set
  • Your workout history — which alarms you completed, snoozed, or skipped — and the streaks calculated from it
  • Your settings, such as language, whether the alarm speaks its message aloud, and the speaking rate

This data is never transmitted to us. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Alarms, notifications, and speech

Reminders are delivered as local notifications scheduled on your device; nothing leaves the device to make an alarm ring. The spoken cheer-up message uses your device's built-in text-to-speech voice. No audio is recorded, and no text is sent to any speech service.

YouTube connection (optional)

In Settings you may optionally connect your Google account so the app can offer you a video from one of your own YouTube playlists after a workout. This is what happens if you connect:

  • Sign-in is handled by Google. The app asks only for read-only access to your YouTube data (the youtube.readonly scope): it can list your playlists and the videos in them, and it cannot create, change, or delete anything in your account.
  • The app uses the sign-in token to call Google's YouTube API directly from your device. Your playlist information is not sent to the developer — there is no developer server to send it to.
  • You can disconnect at any time in Settings, and you can revoke the app's access at any time in your Google Account's security settings.

Google's handling of your account information is described in Google's Privacy Policy. If you never connect YouTube, none of the above takes place.

Children

The app is a general-audience tool designed for older adults. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from anyone else: there is no account, no chat, and no social feature.

Data retention and deletion

Because app data is stored only on your device, we hold no copy of it and there is nothing for us to delete on a server. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data from the device. If you connected YouTube and want to remove that access, disconnect in Settings or revoke it in your Google Account. If you contact us about this policy, any email you send is handled only to answer your question and is not added to any marketing list.
