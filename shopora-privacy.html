Shopora Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Shopora ("the app") is a shopping list, membership card, and receipt management app developed by June Hong. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Data stored on your device

Shopora works entirely on your device. The app does not require an account and does not operate a server that receives your data.

The following information is stored only in the app's local storage on your device:

  • Shopping lists and items you create
  • Membership and loyalty card numbers and barcodes you add
  • Receipt photos and the text read from them (OCR is performed on-device)
  • Monthly budget settings

This data is never transmitted to us. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Camera

The app uses the camera only when you choose to scan a receipt or a membership card barcode. Images are processed on your device and are not uploaded anywhere by the app.

Backups and exports

The export and backup features create files (PDF, CSV, or a password-protected backup file) only when you tap them, and save or share them wherever you choose. The app does not send these files anywhere automatically.

Advertising

Shopora shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use the Android advertising ID and related device information to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and the AdMob data disclosure. This is the only third-party data collection associated with the app.

Children

Shopora is a general shopping utility and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
