SmartShopper

Offline grocery lists with auto-categorization and easy management.

SmartShopper is an intelligent, easy-to-use shopping list app designed to make grocery shopping faster, smarter, and more organized. Items are grouped by category automatically, lists work completely offline, and quick entry keeps your hands free at the store — so you save time and never forget an item again.

Get it on Google Play Download on the App Store

Features

Works Fully Offline

Your lists live on your device. No account, no sign-up, and no connection needed in the store.

Auto-Categorization

Items are grouped by category automatically, so you can shop aisle by aisle without reordering anything by hand.

Multiple Lists

Keep separate lists for different stores, occasions, or family members — and switch between them in one tap.

Quick Add

Rapidly enter items one after another and get back to shopping. Adding to a list takes seconds, not taps through menus.

Simple & Intuitive

A clutter-free interface for adding, editing, checking off, and organizing items with ease.

Private by Design

No account and no cloud sync — your grocery lists stay on your phone, under your control.

Why Choose SmartShopper?

SmartShopper is built for speed and convenience. Whether you are planning a weekly grocery run or organizing a special event, it keeps you on track and stress-free — focus on what matters most: getting your shopping done efficiently.
