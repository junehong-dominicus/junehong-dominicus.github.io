Shopora

Shopping lists, membership cards, and receipts — all in one private app.

Shopora keeps everything about your shopping in one place: the list you are buying from, the loyalty and membership cards you need at checkout, and the receipts you want to keep afterwards. Everything is stored on your device — no account, no cloud, nothing to sign up for.

Get it on Google Play

Features

Shopping Lists

Fast, simple lists for groceries and everyday shopping — add, check off, and reuse them trip after trip.

Membership & Loyalty Cards

Carry your loyalty and membership cards in one wallet, ready to scan at checkout — no more digging through your bag.

Receipts with On-Device OCR

Snap a photo of a receipt and Shopora reads it on your device, so your spending records itself.

Monthly Budget

See where the money went with a clear monthly view of your shopping spend.

PDF / CSV Export

Export your lists and receipts as PDF or CSV whenever you need them for records or sharing.

Password-Protected Backup

Back up and restore your data with password protection, and move it to a new phone on your terms.

Private by Design

No account and no cloud sync. Your lists, cards, and receipts stay on your device, in English or Korean (한국어).

How Shopora Was Built

The idea is mine alone — I built Shopora because I needed it in my own daily life. The detailed development plan and documentation were managed in Confluence, project progress was tracked with Jira tickets, and testing and debugging were carried out with AI agents and coding assistance. It is a small example of a bigger shift: we have entered an era where the apps you need are apps you can build yourself.
