Edge AI Principles

  • Latency is a safety property: When a decision protects people or equipment, it cannot wait for a network. On-device inference keeps reaction time in milliseconds.
  • Constraints first: Model size, memory, and power are design inputs from day one — quantization is an architecture decision, not a compression afterthought.
  • Rules where rules win: Deterministic logic is auditable and predictable; Edge AI earns its place where patterns are too complex to hand-code.
  • Store-and-forward resilience: Edge nodes cache locally and sync when the network returns — the physical system never stops because the cloud did.
